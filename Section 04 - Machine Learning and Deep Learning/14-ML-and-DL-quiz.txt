1. Identify whether you would use supervised, unsupervised or semi supervised learning. You have lots of labeled data that you wish to train the model with. 
(A) Supervised
(B) Unsupervised
(C) Semi Supervised

Answer: A 
2. Identify whether you would use supervised, unsupervised or semi supervised learning. You would like to identify patterns in data but do not know what you are looking for. 
(A) Supervised
(B) Unsupervised
(C) Semi Supervised

Answer: B

(A) Supervised
(B) Unsupervised
(C) Semi Supervised
	
Answer: A

(A) Supervised
(B) Unsupervised
(C) Semi Supervised
	
Answer: B
